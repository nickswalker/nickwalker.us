Reprising [the now-familiar and widely beloved bottle-marathon format](https://drumhellermarathon.com), I hosted the first [North Point Half Marathon](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20046644). The course is 119 laps of the island in [North Point Park](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=18/42.368871/-71.068352). It just so happens that the island is technically within the city of Boston, though the park entrance is in Cambridge. Looking forward to the marathon in years to come.
