When people teach robots by demonstration, they usually can't pay attention to everything at once. Instead of just squeezing the human for more demos to wash this out, what if we could ask for targeted demos or preferences? 

We wrote a [couple]({% details_link merker2026ask %}) [papers]({% details_link merker2026asking %}) on how to do this over the past year, led by Helena Merker. I'll be at CoRL in Austin this November for the latest. Let's run and/or get breakfast tacos.
